03 / Investigation Process & Timelines

Our disciplined 4-stage evidence framework.

Every mandate follows a transparent workflow with defined milestones, ensuring clients understand expected turnaround times from day one.

Stage 01 24 – 48 Hours

Confidential Scoping & Triage

Aligning on the investigative hypothesis, checking bilateral conflicts, executing NDA protections, and defining deliverables.

Milestone: Scoping Charter
Stage 02 5 – 10 Days

Intelligence Gathering & OSINT

Registry retrievals, multi-jurisdictional litigation dockets, asset mapping, and confidential source interviews.

Milestone: Interim Briefing
Stage 03 3 – 5 Days

Corroboration & Testing

Stress-testing leads across independent primary sources to eliminate hearsay, bias, and unsubstantiated allegations.

Milestone: Audit Log
Stage 04 2 – 3 Weeks Total

Executive Dossier & Debrief

Delivering an executive-ready, scannable findings report accompanied by partner-led oral counsel and risk mitigation advice.

Milestone: Certified Dossier
04 / Operating Principles

One rule: a lead is not a finding.

Material conclusions must be corroborated across independent sources and reported with rigorous evidentiary discipline.

Question-Led

We start with the commercial decision you face, identifying what facts must be established rather than producing generic data dumps.

Strictly Corroborated

Every material claim is corroborated across multiple independent primary records before it enters our executive reporting.

Absolute Discretion

Enquiries are conducted on a strict need-to-know basis, ensuring zero market leakage or reputational disruption.
