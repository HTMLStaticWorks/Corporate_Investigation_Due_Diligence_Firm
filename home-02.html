03 / Evidentiary Methodology

The Veritas Investigative Protocol: Phased mandate execution.

Every mandate operates under structured evidentiary stages with verified turnaround intervals, strict chain of custody, and auditable client milestones.

01 Within 24 – 48 Hours

Phase I: Mandate Scoping, Conflict Audit & Bilateral Firewalls

Framing the core investigative question, checking institutional conflicts across all operating offices, executing bilateral non-disclosure protocols, and agreeing on court-defensible evidentiary boundaries.

Operational Deliverable Executed Scope Charter & Conflict Clearance
02 5 – 10 Business Days

Phase II: Multi-Jurisdictional Intelligence & Registry Discovery

Retrieval of non-public and statutory filings across global corporate registries, court dockets, cross-border corporate tree unwinding, and discreet, non-attributable source inquiries.

Operational Deliverable Classified Interim Evidence Dossier
03 3 – 5 Business Days

Phase III: Primary Source Triangulation & Adversarial Corroboration

Stress-testing material findings across independent primary sources to eliminate hearsay, bias, and false trails. Creating verifiable audit logs with document chain-of-custody.

Operational Deliverable Evidentiary Audit Trail & Corroboration Log
04 Final: 2 – 3 Weeks

Phase IV: Counsel-Ready Master Dossier & Partner Oral Debrief

Synthesis into a structured, courtroom-defensible findings dossier designed for executive committees, board chairs, and litigation counsel, accompanied by an in-person or encrypted oral debrief.

Operational Deliverable Defensible Master Dossier & Partner Debrief
