03 / Investigation Process & Timelines

A disciplined four-stage evidence framework.

Every mandate operates with clear turnaround estimates and defined deliverables, ensuring complete transparency.

Stage 01 24 – 48 Hours

Confidential Scoping

Aligning on the investigative hypothesis, executing bilateral NDA protections, and checking client conflicts.

Milestone: Scoping Charter
Stage 02 5 – 10 Days

Intelligence Gathering

Multi-source registry retrieval, litigation dockets, asset mapping, and discreet human intelligence inquiries.

Milestone: Interim Briefing
Stage 03 3 – 5 Days

Corroboration & Testing

Stress-testing leads against independent primary sources to eliminate rumors, bias, and unsubstantiated claims.

Milestone: Audit Log
Stage 04 2 – 3 Weeks Total

Executive Dossier

Delivery of a court-defensible findings dossier accompanied by an oral debrief with corporate decision-makers.

Milestone: Defensible Master Report
