03 / Investigation Framework

Disciplined path from suspicion to evidence.

Every mandate operates under a transparent, 4-stage framework with clear turnaround timelines and verifiable milestones.

Stage 01 24 – 48 Hours

Confidential Scoping & Triage

Framing the core investigative question, executing bilateral NDA protections, checking conflicts, and agreeing on key deliverable parameters.

Milestone: Investigative Scope Charter
Stage 02 5 – 10 Days

Intelligence Gathering & OSINT

Primary registry retrieval, multi-jurisdictional court record mapping, asset tracing, and discreet, non-attributable inquiries.

Milestone: Interim Intelligence Memo
Stage 03 3 – 5 Days

Corroboration & Testing

Challenging material claims across independent primary sources to eliminate rumors, bias, and uncorroborated allegations.

Milestone: Evidentiary Audit Trail
Stage 04 2 – 3 Weeks Total

Executive Dossier & Debrief

Delivery of a court-defensible, scannable findings dossier accompanied by an oral partner-led debrief on commercial next steps.

Milestone: Defensible Master Report
